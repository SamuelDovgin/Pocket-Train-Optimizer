About the plan

New checklists start with your 5 completed additions and the improved graph ownership plan. Your 10 original tracks are also considered built. This is a full-coverage completionist plan with a separately labeled efficiency proxy, not proof that 38 operating trains is economically optimal. You can reassign owners as you progress.

8 newer tracks lack reliable published prices. They display Verify; set their actual cost in the tracker once the game shows it. Price estimates are build + claim and can differ from live game values.
Sources & method: Pocket Trains Wiki — all track prices and station list; updated community map and added links; 26-railroad optimized layout discussion. All 190 listed physical connections are assigned once, and each proposed owner controls a connected subnetwork. Exact best routing depends on engine range and how many railroads you actually want to manage. The Efficiency Lab uses an offline 9,591-job-pair comparison and an editable route planner. The graph optimizer includes exact shortest-path edge betweenness and a connected one/two/three-track relocation heuristic, with caveats for branching and hypothetical job weights.
